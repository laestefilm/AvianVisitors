# Docker illustration prompt (no reference images)

Used by the BirdNET-Go collage adapter when generating species art via Gemini.

Placeholders: `{sci_name}`, `{com_name}`, `{pose}`.

## Prompt

Generate a {pose} {com_name} ({sci_name}) in the style of an Edo-period Japanese kachō-e woodblock print. Confident sumi-e ink linework with soft watercolor washes. Earthy, restrained palette: burnt umber, ochre, indigo, vermillion, muted greens. The body should look like flat painted paper with 2-4 flat color zones and sharp boundaries — not feather-by-feather texture.

The bird sits on a CONSISTENT WARM CREAM tonal background — like aged Japanese mulberry paper. The cream ground fills the entire frame. NO branch, NO twig, NO perch, NO leaves, NO scenery, NO border, NO text.

Composition: the bird occupies one-third to one-half of the frame with generous negative space. The ENTIRE bird must fit within the image frame with padding on all sides — do not crop wings, tail, legs, or beak.

Anatomy: exactly two wings, two legs, one head, one beak, one tail. Match field-guide proportions and diagnostic markings for {com_name}. Both feet visible at the bottom with natural songbird proportions unless this is a larger species.

PERCHED: one wing folded, feet visible, toes curled as if grasping an invisible perch.
IN FLIGHT: both wings extended; legs tucked or extended back along the tail line.

Render at high resolution on a fully transparent background. Cut the bird out cleanly. No shadow, no paper texture, no caption.
